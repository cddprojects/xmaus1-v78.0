Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about remote work opportunities.

Is experience required?

No. The platform uses a standard flow and we provide full onboarding training. We’ll guide you step by step through the trial period.

Is this a sales role?

No, you don't need to handle sales, recruitment, or negotiations. You simply need to carry out project tasks according to the standard process.

What are the working hours?

Anytime during the day works. Most finish daily tasks quickly, with no fixed duration.

Is there any cost to join?

There is no joining fee for the application or onboarding training. The trial lets you confirm the role fits before continuing.

How is the reward calculated?

The reward depends on the quantity and quality of tasks completed; there is no fixed guaranteed base reward..
